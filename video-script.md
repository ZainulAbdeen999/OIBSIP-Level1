# Demo video script

Total time: about 2 minutes. Record at 1080p, landscape, 30fps.

Before you record:

- Open `title-card.html` in the browser. Press `1`, `2` or `3` to pick the task, or
  open `title-card.html?task=1`. Press `F` for fullscreen if the browser does not
  hide the toolbar with `F11`.
- Zoom the browser to 110 percent so the text is readable.
- Close other tabs, turn off notifications, and check your mic once.

Say the lines in your own words if you want. The important part is that the first
two seconds show your name, the track and the task title on screen.

> "Hi, I'm Zain ul Abdeen, and this is Task 1 of my Web Development and Designing
> internship at Oasis Infobyte. The code for all three Level 1 tasks is in one public

**Show:** GitHub repository page, scroll the folder list.

**Show:** `OIBSIP/WebDevelopment-L1-Task1-LandingPage/index.html`, desktop width.

> "If the input is not a number, it says so instead of giving a wrong answer. I check

> "And anything below absolute zero gets a warning. The limit is different for each

## If you need three separate videos

Use the same lines and stop after each task. Each video still needs the title card in
the first two seconds, with the correct task number selected.

- Video 1: opening, repository, Task 1. About 50 seconds.
- Video 2: opening line with Task 2, then Task 2. About 40 seconds.
- Video 3: opening line with Task 3, then Task 3. About 45 seconds.

## Recording tips

- Slow down. It is normal to speak slower on video than in a conversation.
- Move the mouse slowly and do not wiggle it while talking.
- If you make a mistake, keep going. A small stumble looks more real than a perfect
  read.
- Watch your own video once before uploading. Check that the first frame shows your
  name and that the audio is not too quiet.
