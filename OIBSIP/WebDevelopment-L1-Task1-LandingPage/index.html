Our story

Started in a kitchen, still small on purpose

Kohva began with a second-hand drum roaster and a stubborn idea: coffee should taste like the people and places behind it. Seven years later we still roast in the same small batches, just for more kitchens.

We work directly with six family farms across Ethiopia, Colombia and Guatemala, paying above fair-trade rates and publishing what we pay.
